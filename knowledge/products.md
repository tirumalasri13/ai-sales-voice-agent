# SunNest Solar Products

## Residential rooftop solar
Our residential systems range from 2 kW to 10 kW and suit homes with a monthly electricity bill of Rs 2,000 or more. A 3 kW system powers a typical family home with a fridge, fans, lights, TV and two air conditioners. Most homes need about 300 square feet of shadow-free roof for 3 kW.

## Commercial and industrial solar
Shops, schools, factories and farms can install 10 kW to 500 kW systems. Commercial customers get accelerated depreciation tax benefits, and the investment usually pays back in 3 to 4 years.

## Solar water pumps
We offer 3 HP and 5 HP solar pumps for farmers with up to 90 percent government subsidy support in eligible states. Pumps run without any electricity or diesel cost.

## Battery backup
Optional lithium battery packs of 5 kWh and 10 kWh keep your home running during power cuts. Batteries add roughly Rs 60,000 to Rs 1,20,000 to the system price.
