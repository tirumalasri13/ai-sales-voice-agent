# Service and FAQ

## Installation timeline
Installation takes 2 to 3 days after the site survey and design approval. The complete process including net-meter approval usually takes 3 to 4 weeks.

## Warranty
Panels carry a 25 year performance warranty and the inverter carries a 10 year warranty. We provide a 5 year free maintenance plan with cleaning visits twice a year.

## Service area
We currently serve Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana, Delhi NCR and Rajasthan, including Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Guntur, Tirupati, Warangal, Ludhiana, Jalandhar, Amritsar, Chandigarh and Mohali.

## Free site survey
Our engineer visits your property free of charge, checks roof space, shadow and load, and gives an exact quotation on the same day. The survey takes about 45 minutes and there is no obligation to buy.

## Monsoon and maintenance
Panels work on cloudy days at reduced output of 20 to 30 percent. Rain naturally cleans the panels. We monitor performance through a mobile app and alert you if output drops.
