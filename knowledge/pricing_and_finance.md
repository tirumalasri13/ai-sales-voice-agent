# Pricing

## System pricing
A 3 kW rooftop system costs about Rs 1,80,000 before subsidy. A 5 kW system costs about Rs 3,00,000 before subsidy. Price includes panels, inverter, mounting structure, wiring, net-meter paperwork and installation.

## Government subsidy
Under the PM Surya Ghar scheme, households get a central subsidy of up to Rs 78,000 for systems of 3 kW and above. We handle the subsidy application end to end, and the amount is credited to your bank account after installation and inspection.

## Financing and EMI
Zero-down-payment EMI is available through partner banks with tenures from 3 to 7 years. For a 3 kW system the EMI is often lower than the monthly electricity bill it replaces. Payback and savings: most homes recover the investment in 4 to 5 years and save for 25 years afterwards.

## Savings estimate
A home with a Rs 4,000 monthly bill typically saves 80 to 90 percent of the bill after installing a 3 kW system. Net metering lets you sell extra power back to the grid and earn credits.
